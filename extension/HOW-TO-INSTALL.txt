Scraper.co.in — RAM Turbo extension
===================================

This ZIP is only for local development. Public users install the extension
from the Chrome Web Store with one click.

Local development (3 steps)
---------------------------
1. Unzip this file. Keep the whole folder named:
   Scraper-RAM-Turbo-Extension
   Do NOT open manifest.json.

2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on "Developer mode" (top right).

3. Click "Load unpacked" (top left) and choose the whole
   Scraper-RAM-Turbo-Extension folder — not a file inside it.

Then open http://localhost:5173/command and refresh once. The extension
connects itself and shows your real RAM and CPU cores.

What it can read
----------------
Only the page the server asks for, from the 47 named search, maps and
directory sites for your 11 countries. It never reads your other tabs,
history, passwords or personal data.

What lives where
----------------
- Our 6 engines, geo data and phone checks stay on our secure cloud.
- The extension only adds your connection and spare RAM for speed.
